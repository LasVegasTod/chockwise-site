Thanksgiving flying questions

What is the best time of day to fly at Thanksgiving?

Early. Departures scheduled before 9 am left the gate on time 90% of the time; after 5 pm, 68%. A delay early in the day follows the plane to every later flight.

Which airline is most on time?

Volaris left on time most often (88% of departures), British Airways least often (66%), among airlines with 50+ departures a day.

Which airport has the most on-time departures?

Of the 49 airports with 100+ departures a day, New York JFK (JFK) left on time most often (86%) and San Diego (SAN) least often (55%).

What is the busiest airport gate in the US?

The busiest gate we measured is A12 at Seattle (SEA): about 15 departures a day, held by Delta Air Lines.

Where do these numbers come from?

Every airline arrival and departure at 119 US airports from FlightAware, worked out by Chockwise. Each airport's latest full days of normal flying (Sep 25 to Oct 8, 2026). On time = left the gate less than 15 minutes after the scheduled time, the line the US Department of Transportation uses.
