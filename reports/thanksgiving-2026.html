Thanksgiving flying questions

What is the best time of day to fly at Thanksgiving?

Early. Departures scheduled before 9 am left the gate on time 90% of the time; after 5 pm, 69%. A delay early in the day follows the plane to every later flight.

Which airline is most on time?

Breeze Airways left on time most often (84% of departures), Southwest Airlines least often (65%), among US airlines with 50+ departures a day.

Which airport has the most on-time departures?

Of the 55 airports with 100+ departures a day, Istanbul (IST) left on time most often (87%) and San Diego (SAN) least often (57%).

What is the busiest airport gate in the US?

The busiest gate we measured is 1A at Jeju (CJU): about 17 departures a day, held by Jeju Air.

Where do these numbers come from?

Every airline arrival and departure at 236 US airports from FlightAware, worked out by Chockwise. Each airport's latest full days of normal flying (Sep 25 to Oct 20, 2026). On time = left the gate less than 15 minutes after the scheduled time, the line the US Department of Transportation uses.
