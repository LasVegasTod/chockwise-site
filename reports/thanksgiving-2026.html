Thanksgiving flying questions

What is the best time of day to fly at Thanksgiving?

Early. Departures scheduled before 9 am left the gate on time 88% of the time; after 5 pm, 68%. A delay early in the day follows the plane to every later flight.

Which airline is most on time?

United Airlines left on time most often (80% of departures), Southwest Airlines least often (67%), among US airlines with 50+ departures a day.

Which airport has the most on-time departures?

Of the 46 airports with 100+ departures a day, Pittsburgh (PIT) left on time most often (85%) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL) least often (61%).

What is the busiest airport gate in the US?

The busiest gate we measured is 9 at Kona (KOA): about 13 departures a day, held by Alaska Airlines.

Where do these numbers come from?

Every airline arrival and departure at 190 US airports from FlightAware, worked out by Chockwise. Each airport's latest full days of normal flying (Oct 1 to Nov 23, 2026). On time = left the gate less than 15 minutes after the scheduled time, the line the US Department of Transportation uses.
