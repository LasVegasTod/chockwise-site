Where does Jetstar Japan fly from? Terminals and gates at 6 airports

Jetstar Japan is built around Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT): 8 of its 9 gates (89%) are there, in Terminal 3. Jetstar Japan is the biggest airline at NRT by departures. Jetstar Japan’s median turn at NRT is 56 min, 33 min shorter than the airport median.

Top airports

At Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT), Jetstar Japan has 8 gates of its own in Terminal 3 and runs about 39 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Osaka Kansai Airport (KIX), Jetstar Japan has 1 gate of its own in Terminal 1 and Terminal 1C and runs about 8-9 departures a day.

At Fukuoka Airport (FUK), Jetstar Japan uses shared gates in Terminal 3, about 10 departures a day.

At Sapporo Airport (CTS), Jetstar Japan uses shared gates in Terminal D, about 10 departures a day.

At Taipei Taoyuan Airport (TPE), Jetstar Japan uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses B, C and A, about 4 departures a day.
