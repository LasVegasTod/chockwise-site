Where does Cathay Pacific fly from? Terminals and gates at 31 airports

Cathay Pacific is built around Hong Kong Airport (HKG): 17 of its 21 gates (81%) are there, in Terminal 1. Cathay Pacific is the biggest airline at HKG by departures. Cathay Pacific’s median turn at HKG is 134 min, 26 min longer than the airport median.

Top airports

At Hong Kong Airport (HKG), Cathay Pacific has 17 gates of its own in Terminal 1 and runs about 191 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Singapore Airport (SIN), Cathay Pacific has 3 gates of its own in Terminal 4’s Concourse G and runs about 8-9 departures a day.

At O’Hare Airport (ORD), Cathay Pacific has 1 gate of its own in Terminal 5 and runs about 2 departures a day.

At Taipei Taoyuan Airport (TPE), Cathay Pacific uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourse B, about 16 departures a day, 5th of 57 airlines.

At Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), Cathay Pacific uses shared gates in Concourses G, E, F and D, about 8 departures a day.

At Beijing Capital Airport (PEK), Cathay Pacific uses shared gates in Terminal 3’s Concourse E, about 7-8 departures a day.

At Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT), Cathay Pacific uses shared gates in Terminal 2, about 6-7 departures a day.

At Osaka Kansai Airport (KIX), Cathay Pacific uses shared gates in Terminal 1E, about 6 departures a day.

When it flies

Its earliest departure across its top airports is around 5:00 am at HKG. The busiest single hour is 8:00 am–9:00 am at HKG (~33 arrivals and departures). About 20 of its aircraft spend the night at HKG.

Routes and aircraft

From HKG, Cathay Pacific flies nonstop to 79 destinations, the busiest Taipei (TPE, 12/day), Shanghai (PVG, 9/day), Singapore (SIN, 8-9/day) and Bangkok (BKK, 8-9/day). At HKG it flies the A330-300 (62/day, 262 seats), 777-300ER (27/day, 340 seats) and A321neo (26/day, 202 seats).
