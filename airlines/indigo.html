Where does IndiGo fly from? Terminals and gates at 11 airports

IndiGo is built around Mumbai Airport (BOM): 15 of its 15 gates (100%) are there, in Terminal 2 and Terminal 1. IndiGo is the biggest airline at BOM by departures. No IndiGo flights at Jakarta Airport (CGK) since Oct 15, 2026.

Top airports

At Mumbai Airport (BOM), IndiGo has 15 gates of its own in Terminal 2 and Terminal 1 and runs about 185 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Dubai Airport (DXB), IndiGo uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourse D, about 15 departures a day, 3rd of 56 airlines.

At Singapore Airport (SIN), IndiGo uses shared gates in Terminal 2’s Concourses F and E, about 11 departures a day, 5th of 87 airlines.

At Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), IndiGo uses shared gates in Concourses F, D, G and E, about 9-10 departures a day.

At Doha Airport (DOH), IndiGo uses shared gates in Concourses B and A, about 9 departures a day, 2nd of 34 airlines.
