Where does Qantas fly from? Terminals and gates at 18 airports

Qantas is built around Melbourne Airport (MEL): 15 of its 28 gates (54%) are there, in Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. Qantas is the biggest airline at MEL and BNE by departures. Qantas’ median turn at MEL is 64 min, 6 min shorter than the airport median.

Top airports

At Melbourne Airport (MEL), Qantas has 15 gates of its own in Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 and runs about 106 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Brisbane Airport (BNE), Qantas has 13 gates of its own in Terminal D and Terminal I and runs about 117 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Auckland Airport (AKL), Qantas uses shared gates in Terminal 1, about 14 departures a day, 3rd of 22 airlines.

At Singapore Airport (SIN), Qantas uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses D and C, about 10 departures a day.

At Los Angeles Airport (LAX), Qantas uses shared gates in Terminal B, about 3 departures a day.
