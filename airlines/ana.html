Where does ANA fly from? Terminals and gates at 25 airports

All Nippon Airways (ANA) is built around Tokyo Haneda Airport (HND): 27 of its 42 gates (64%) are there, in Terminal 2N, Terminal 2S, Terminal 2, Terminal 1S and Terminal 3. ANA is the biggest airline at HND, CTS and FUK by departures.

Top airports

At Tokyo Haneda Airport (HND), ANA has 27 gates of its own in Terminal 2N, Terminal 2S, Terminal 2, Terminal 1S and Terminal 3 and runs about 278 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT), ANA has 9 gates of its own in Terminal 1 and runs about 18 departures a day, 4th of 75 airlines.

At Sapporo Airport (CTS), ANA has 4 gates of its own in Terminal D and runs about 73 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Fukuoka Airport (FUK), ANA has 1 gate of its own in Terminal 2 and Terminal 3 and runs about 46 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Osaka Kansai Airport (KIX), ANA has 1 gate of its own in Terminal 1 and Terminal 1D and runs about 10 departures a day, 4th of 65 airlines.

At Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), ANA uses shared gates in Concourses E, G, D and F, about 5-6 departures a day.

At Singapore Airport (SIN), ANA uses shared gates in Terminal 2’s Concourses E and F, about 3-4 departures a day.

At Hong Kong Airport (HKG), ANA uses shared gates in Terminal 1, about 3-4 departures a day.
