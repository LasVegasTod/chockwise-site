Where does Emirates fly from? Terminals and gates at 44 airports

Emirates is built around Dubai Airport (DXB): 75 of its 79 gates (95%) are there, in Terminal 3’s Concourses A, B and C. Emirates is the biggest airline at DXB by departures. Emirates’ median turn at DXB is 180 min, 50 min longer than the airport median.

Top airports

At Dubai Airport (DXB), Emirates has 75 gates of its own in Terminal 3’s Concourses A, B and C and runs about 224 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Athens Airport (ATH), Emirates has 2 gates of its own in Terminal 1’s Concourse A and runs about 3 departures a day.

At Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP), Emirates has 1 gate of its own in Terminal 1’s Concourse B and runs about 4-5 departures a day.

At Frankfurt Airport (FRA), Emirates has 1 gate of its own in Terminal 3’s Concourse J and runs about 4 departures a day.

At Hong Kong Airport (HKG), Emirates uses shared gates in Terminal 1, about 8 departures a day.

At Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), Emirates uses shared gates in Concourse S, Terminal 3’s Concourse S and Terminal 1’s Concourse S, about 7 departures a day.

At London Heathrow Airport (LHR), Emirates uses shared gates in Terminal 3, about 6 departures a day.

At Singapore Airport (SIN), Emirates uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses C and D, about 5-6 departures a day.

When it flies

Its earliest departure across its top airports is around 4:00 am at DXB. The busiest single hour is 8:00 am–9:00 am at DXB (~44 arrivals and departures). About 14 of its aircraft spend the night at DXB.

Routes and aircraft

From DXB, Emirates flies nonstop to 129 destinations, the busiest London Heathrow (LHR, 6/day), Bangkok (BKK, 5/day), Cairo (CAI, 5/day) and Mumbai (BOM, 5/day). At DXB it flies the 777-300ER (115/day, 354 seats), A380 (68/day, 489 seats) and A350-900 (32/day, 312 seats).

About this data
