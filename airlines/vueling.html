Where does Vueling fly from? Terminals and gates at 16 airports

Vueling is built around Barcelona Airport (BCN): 17 of its 17 gates (100%) are there, in Terminal 1’s Concourses B, A, D and C. Vueling is the biggest airline at BCN by departures. No Vueling flights at Stockholm Airport (ARN) since Nov 1, 2026.

Top airports

At Barcelona Airport (BCN), Vueling has 17 gates of its own in Terminal 1’s Concourses B, A, D and C and runs about 194 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI), Vueling uses shared gates in Terminal N’s Concourse D, about 25 departures a day, 4th of 57 airlines.

At Amsterdam Airport (AMS), Vueling uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses C and B, about 10 departures a day.

At Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP), Vueling uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourse A, about 8-9 departures a day, 4th of 88 airlines.

When it flies

Its earliest departure across its top airports is around 5:40 am at BCN. The busiest single hour is 7:00 am–8:00 am at BCN (~36 arrivals and departures). About 13 of its aircraft spend the night at BCN.

Routes and aircraft

From BCN, Vueling flies nonstop to 94 destinations, the busiest Palma de Mallorca (PMI, 10/day), Paris Orly (ORY, 7-8/day), Sevilla (SVQ, 7/day) and Milan (MXP, 6-7/day). At BCN it flies the A320 (136/day, 180 seats), A321 (38/day) and A321neo (8-9/day, 220 seats).
