Where does Peach fly from? Terminals and gates at 8 airports

Peach Aviation is built around Osaka Kansai Airport (KIX): 15 of its 15 gates (100%) are there, in Terminal 2. Peach is the biggest airline at KIX by departures.

Top airports

At Osaka Kansai Airport (KIX), Peach has 15 gates of its own in Terminal 2 and runs about 45 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT), Peach uses shared gates in Terminal 1, about 26 departures a day, 3rd of 75 airlines.

At Taipei Taoyuan Airport (TPE), Peach uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses A and B, about 11 departures a day.

At Seoul Incheon Airport (ICN), Peach uses shared gates in Terminal 1, about 6-7 departures a day.

At Tokyo Haneda Airport (HND), Peach uses shared gates in Terminal 3, about 3 departures a day.
