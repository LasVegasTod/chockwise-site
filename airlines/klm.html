Where does KLM fly from? Terminals and gates at 50 airports

When it flies

Its earliest departure across its top airports is around 6:00 am at BER. The busiest single hour is 8:00 am–9:00 am at AMS (~64 arrivals and departures). About 26 of its aircraft spend the night at AMS.

From AMS, KLM flies nonstop to 167 destinations, the busiest London Heathrow (LHR, 7/day), Berlin (BER, 6-7/day), London (LCY, 6/day) and Prague (PRG, 5-6/day). At AMS it flies the E195 (63/day), E190 (59/day) and E175 (51/day).
