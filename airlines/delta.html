Where does Delta fly from? Terminals and gates at 172 airports

Delta Air Lines spreads 1,021 gates across 172 airports, with no airport having more than 15% of them. Its biggest are Atlanta Airport (ATL), with 149, and Detroit Airport (DTW), with 81. Delta is the biggest airline at ATL, DTW, MSP and 16 more by departures.
