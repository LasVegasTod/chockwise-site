United: questions

Where does United fly from?

United Airlines flies from 172 airports that Chockwise tracks. The biggest are O’Hare Airport (ORD), in Terminal 1 & 2; Houston Bush Airport (IAH), in Terminal B & C; and Denver Airport (DEN), in Concourses A & B.

Which terminal is United at in O’Hare?

United flies from Terminal 2’s Concourses F (28% of its departures) and E (15%), Terminal 1’s Concourses C (27%) and B (25%) and Terminal 3’s Concourse G (4%) at O’Hare Airport (ORD).

Which terminal is United at in Houston Bush?

United flies from Terminal C’s Concourse C (36% of its departures), Terminal B’s Concourse B (28%), Terminal E’s Concourse E (23%), Terminal A’s Concourse A (6%) and Terminal D’s Concourse D (5%) at Houston Bush Airport (IAH).

How many gates does United have at ORD?

United has 94 gates of its own at O’Hare Airport (ORD).

How many flights a day does United have from ORD?
