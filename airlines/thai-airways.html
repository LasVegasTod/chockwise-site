Where does Thai Airways fly from? Terminals and gates at 24 airports

Thai Airways is built around Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK): 10 of its 10 gates (100%) are there, in Concourses S, C, B, D and A. Thai Airways is the biggest airline at BKK by departures. Thai Airways’ median turn at BKK is 127 min, 41 min longer than the airport median.

Routes and aircraft

From BKK, Thai Airways flies nonstop to 66 destinations, the busiest CNX (8-9/day), Phuket (HKT, 8/day), Singapore (SIN, 5/day) and Hong Kong (HKG, 4/day). At BKK it flies the A320 (52/day), A350-900 (16/day) and 777-300ER (12/day).
