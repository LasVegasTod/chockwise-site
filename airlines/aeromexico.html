Where does Aeroméxico fly from? Terminals and gates at 24 airports

Aeroméxico is built around Mexico City Airport (MEX): 25 of its 28 gates (89%) are there, in Terminal 2. Aeroméxico is the biggest airline at MEX by departures. No Aeroméxico flights at Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI) since Oct 18, 2026.

Top airports

At Mexico City Airport (MEX), Aeroméxico has 25 gates of its own in Terminal 2 and runs about 168 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Miami Airport (MIA), Aeroméxico has 1 gate of its own in Concourses H and J and runs about 2-3 departures a day.

At Orlando Airport (MCO), Aeroméxico has 1 gate of its own in Terminal C and runs about 2 departures a day.

At Houston Bush Airport (IAH), Aeroméxico has 1 gate of its own in Terminal D’s Concourse D and runs about 1-2 departures a day.

At Los Angeles Airport (LAX), Aeroméxico uses shared gates in Terminal 3, about 5 departures a day.

At New York JFK Airport, Aeroméxico uses shared gates in Terminal 4, about 2-3 departures a day.

At O’Hare Airport (ORD), Aeroméxico uses shared gates in Terminal 5, about 2 departures a day.

At San Francisco Airport (SFO), Aeroméxico uses shared gates in Terminal I’s Concourse A, about 1-2 departures a day.

In all: 24 airports, 28 gates of its own and about 200 departures a day (added up across them). The most nonstop destinations from one airport: 75, from MEX. At MEX it has 43% of departures, about 3 times Volaris’ 15%.

When it flies

Its earliest departure across its top airports is around 5:55 am at MIA. The busiest single hour is 4:00 pm–5:00 pm at MEX (~23 arrivals and departures). About 23 of its aircraft spend the night at MEX.

Routes and aircraft

From MEX, Aeroméxico flies nonstop to 75 destinations, the busiest Monterrey (MTY, 12/day), Guadalajara (GDL, 11/day), Cancún (CUN, 11/day) and Mérida (MID, 7-8/day). At MEX it flies the 737 MAX 8 (72/day, 166 seats), 737 MAX 9 (57/day) and 737-800 (20/day).
