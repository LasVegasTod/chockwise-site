Where does SAS fly from? Terminals and gates at 33 airports

SAS Scandinavian Airlines is built around Copenhagen Airport (CPH): 24 of its 44 gates (55%) are there, in Terminal 3’s Concourses B, E, A, D and C. SAS is the biggest airline at CPH and ARN by departures. SAS’s median turn at CPH is 63 min, 9 min longer than the airport median.

Top airports

At Copenhagen Airport (CPH), SAS has 24 gates of its own in Terminal 3’s Concourses B, E, A, D and C and runs about 182 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Stockholm Airport (ARN), SAS has 16 gates of its own in Terminal 5’s Concourses E and F and runs about 114 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Oslo Airport (OSL), SAS has 4 gates of its own in Concourses A, C, E, B and F and runs about 96 departures a day, 2nd of 40 airlines.

At London Heathrow Airport (LHR), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal 2, about 13 departures a day.

At Amsterdam Airport (AMS), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses C and B, about 9 departures a day.

At Helsinki Airport (HEL), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal 2, about 6-7 departures a day, 4th of 20 airlines.

At Málaga Airport (AGP), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal PAX’s Concourse D, about 6 departures a day.

At Berlin Airport (BER), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses A and B, about 4-5 departures a day.

In all: 33 airports, 44 gates of its own and about 473 departures a day (added up across them). The most nonstop destinations from one airport: 110, from CPH. At CPH it has 45% of departures, about 4 times Norwegian Air’s 12%.

When it flies

Its earliest departure across its top airports is around 5:50 am at OSL. The busiest single hour is 8:00 am–9:00 am at CPH (~42 arrivals and departures). About 15 of its aircraft spend the night at CPH.

Routes and aircraft

From CPH, SAS flies nonstop to 110 destinations, the busiest Stockholm (ARN, 13/day), Oslo (OSL, 12/day), AAL (5-6/day) and London Heathrow (LHR, 5/day). At CPH it flies the A320neo (51/day, 180 seats), CRJ-900 (34/day) and A320 (24/day).

About this data

Based on 650 full days of flights at CPH, ARN, OSL and LHR and more, Oct 4 – Nov 11, 2026. Gates of its own are the gates where SAS flies the most (regional partners count as their brand). 63% of its departures have no seat count yet, so seat numbers run low.
