Where does SAS fly from? Terminals and gates at 25 airports

SAS Scandinavian Airlines is built around Copenhagen Airport (CPH): 23 of its 43 gates (53%) are there, in Terminal 3’s Concourses B, E, A, D and C. SAS is the biggest airline at CPH and ARN by departures. SAS’s median turn at CPH is 64 min, 10 min longer than the airport median.

Top airports

At Copenhagen Airport (CPH), SAS has 23 gates of its own in Terminal 3’s Concourses B, E, A, D and C and runs about 184 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Stockholm Airport (ARN), SAS has 14 gates of its own in Terminal 5’s Concourses E and F and runs about 114 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Oslo Airport (OSL), SAS has 6 gates of its own in Concourses A, C, E, F and B and runs about 96 departures a day, 2nd of 37 airlines.

At London Heathrow Airport (LHR), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal 2, about 14 departures a day.

At Amsterdam Airport (AMS), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses C and B, about 9-10 departures a day.

At Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourse A, about 5 departures a day.

At Athens Airport (ATH), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal 4’s Concourse B, about 3-4 departures a day.

At Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI), SAS uses shared gates in Terminal N’s Concourse C, about 3-4 departures a day.

When it flies

Its earliest departure across its top airports is around 5:50 am at ARN. The busiest single hour is 8:00 am–9:00 am at CPH (~43 arrivals and departures). About 13 of its aircraft spend the night at CPH.

Routes and aircraft
