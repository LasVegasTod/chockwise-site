Where does Ryanair fly from? Terminals and gates at 8 airports

Ryanair is built around Dublin Airport (DUB): 15 of its 23 gates (65%) are there, in Terminal 1. Ryanair is the biggest airline at DUB and PMI by departures. Ryanair’s median turn at DUB is 45 min, 9 min shorter than the airport median.

Top airports

At Dublin Airport (DUB), Ryanair has 15 gates of its own in Terminal 1 and runs about 136 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Vienna Airport (VIE), Ryanair has 5 gates of its own in Terminal 1’s Concourses C and D and runs about 50 departures a day, 2nd of 59 airlines.

At Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI), Ryanair has 2 gates of its own in Terminal N’s Concourses C and A and runs about 96 departures a day, the most of any airline there.

At Copenhagen Airport (CPH), Ryanair has 1 gate of its own in Terminal 2’s Concourse F and runs about 28 departures a day, 3rd of 61 airlines.

At Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP), Ryanair uses shared gates in Terminal 1’s Concourses A and B, about 49 departures a day, 2nd of 85 airlines.
