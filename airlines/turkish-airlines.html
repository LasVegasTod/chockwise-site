Where does Turkish Airlines fly from? Terminals and gates at 43 airports

Turkish Airlines is built around Istanbul Airport (IST): 83 of its 83 gates (100%) are there, in Concourses F, G, B, D, A, E and C. Turkish Airlines is the biggest airline at IST by departures. Turkish Airlines’ median turn at IST is 112 min, 10 min longer than the airport median.

When it flies

Its earliest departure across its top airports is around 4:55 am at IST. The busiest single hour is 2:00 pm–3:00 pm at IST (~72 arrivals and departures). About 59 of its aircraft spend the night at IST.

Routes and aircraft

From IST, Turkish Airlines flies nonstop to 255 destinations, the busiest Antalya (AYT, 15/day), Ankara (ESB, 14/day), İzmir (ADB, 13/day) and Tarsus (COV, 9-10/day). At IST it flies the A321neo (107/day, 182 seats), 737-800 (102/day, 165 seats) and A321 (93/day).
