Nonstop routes and daily frequency from any US airport

Chockwise tracks 13,372 nonstop routes from 237 airports, flown by 550 airlines, from departures seen Sep 25 – Oct 22, 2026. Type an airport to list every destination from it, each with the airlines that fly it and how often; add a second airport to see who flies that one route. Flights a day are each airline’s departures on the route over the airport’s full days of data, averaged; a route flown less than once a day shows as flights a week.       Route finder: questions

      How do I see every nonstop route from an airport?

      Type its code or city in From. The list shows every destination with flights a day or a week and each airline on it. Chockwise has routes for 237 airports.

      How is flights a day calculated?

      Each airline’s departures on a route are counted over the airport’s full days of data and averaged; under one a day is shown per week, so 2 flights in 7 days reads 2 a week.

      Which airport has the most nonstop routes?

      Amsterdam Airport (AMS), with 293 nonstop destinations in the data (Oct 5–22, 2026).

      How often is the route data updated?

      With every new day of flights; the busiest airports add a day each night. This data runs to Oct 22, 2026.
