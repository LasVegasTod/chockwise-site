How many gates does Hong Kong Airport (HKG) have?

Hong Kong Airport (HKG) has 91 gates, with about 568 airline departures a day. Cathay Pacific has the most (17), followed by HK Express (2).

Cathay Pacific flies 56% of the 66,052 seats leaving HKG each day. Cathay Pacific has 750% more gates and 240% more seats than HK Express. 66 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
