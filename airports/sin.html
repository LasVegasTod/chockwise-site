How many gates does Singapore Airport (SIN) have?

Singapore Airport (SIN) has 131 gates, with about 526 airline departures a day. Singapore Airlines has the most (23), followed by Scoot (14).

Singapore Airlines flies 52% of the 69,133 seats leaving SIN each day. Singapore Airlines has 64% more gates and 95% more seats than Scoot. 62 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
