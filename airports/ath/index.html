How many gates does Athens Airport (ATH) have?

Athens Airport (ATH) has 51 gates, with about 379 airline departures a day. Aegean has the most (6), followed by Emirates (1).

Aegean flies 23% of the 18,787 seats leaving ATH each day. Aegean has 500% more gates and 347% more seats than Emirates. 36 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
