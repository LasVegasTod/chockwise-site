How many gates does Oslo Airport (OSL) have?

Oslo Airport (OSL) has 55 gates, with about 315 airline departures a day. Norwegian has the most (7), followed by SAS (5).

Norwegian flies 55% of the 30,803 seats leaving OSL each day. Norwegian has 40% more gates and 83% more seats than SAS. 40 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
