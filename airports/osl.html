How many gates does Oslo Airport (OSL) have?

Oslo Airport (OSL) has 55 gates, with about 288 airline departures a day. Norwegian and SAS have the most, 6 each.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 23, 2026 · 10 full days from Oct 27.

Norwegian flies 48% of the 26,197 seats leaving OSL each day. Norwegian has 35% more seats than SAS. 40 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
