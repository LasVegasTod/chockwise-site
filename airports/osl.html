How many gates does Oslo Airport (OSL) have?

Oslo Airport (OSL) has 55 gates, with about 305 airline departures a day. Norwegian has the most (7), followed by SAS (4).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 9, 2026 · 22 full days from Oct 13.

Norwegian flies 52% of the 29,240 seats leaving OSL each day. Norwegian has 75% more gates and 63% more seats than SAS. 42 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
