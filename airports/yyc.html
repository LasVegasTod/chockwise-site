How many gates does Calgary Airport (YYC) have?

Calgary Airport (YYC) has 58 gates, with about 242 airline departures a day. WestJet has the most (31), followed by Air Canada (4).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 2, 2026 · 2 full days from Nov 1.

WestJet flies 75% of the 22,659 seats leaving YYC each day. WestJet has 7.8 times as many gates and 4.4 times as many seats as Air Canada. 14 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
