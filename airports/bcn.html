How many gates does Barcelona Airport (BCN) have?

Barcelona Airport (BCN) has 108 gates, with about 566 airline departures a day. Vueling has the most (17), followed by easyJet (4).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 16, 2026 · 16 full days from Oct 20.

Vueling flies 38% of the 73,416 seats leaving BCN each day. Vueling has 4.2 times as many gates and 5.9 times as many seats as easyJet. 64 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
