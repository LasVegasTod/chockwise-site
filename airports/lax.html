How many gates does Los Angeles Airport (LAX) have?

Los Angeles Airport (LAX) has 148 gates, with about 700 airline departures a day. Delta has the most (25), followed by United (24).

Delta flies 23% of the 109,223 seats leaving LAX each day. Delta has 4% more gates and 5% more seats than United. 31 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
