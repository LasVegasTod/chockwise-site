How many gates does Anchorage Airport (ANC) have?

Anchorage Airport (ANC) has 26 gates, with about 102 airline departures a day. Alaska has the most (12), followed by Delta (2).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Oct 5, 2026 · 3 full days from Oct 3.

Alaska flies 79% of the 8,736 seats leaving ANC each day. Alaska has 6.0 times as many gates and 5.8 times as many seats as Delta. 1 gate is common use, shared by several airlines.
