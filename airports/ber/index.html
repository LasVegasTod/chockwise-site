How many gates does Berlin Airport (BER) have?

Berlin Airport (BER) has 43 gates, with about 284 airline departures a day. Ryanair has 4 gates.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 2, 2026 · 26 full days from Oct 8.

Ryanair flies 24% of the 25,915 seats leaving BER each day. 36 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
