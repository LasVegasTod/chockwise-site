How many gates does Berlin Airport (BER) have?

Berlin Airport (BER) has 43 gates, with about 275 airline departures a day. Ryanair has 1 gate.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 16, 2026 · 16 full days from Oct 20.

Ryanair flies 22% of the 24,521 seats leaving BER each day. 35 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
