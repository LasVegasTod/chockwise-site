How many gates does Brisbane Airport (BNE) have?

Brisbane Airport (BNE) has 58 gates, with about 307 airline departures a day. Qantas has the most (13), followed by Virgin Australia (10).

Qantas flies 28% of the 29,882 seats leaving BNE each day. Qantas has 30% more gates than Virgin Australia. 26 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
