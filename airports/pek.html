How many gates does Beijing Capital Airport (PEK) have?

Beijing Capital Airport (PEK) has 123 gates, with about 588 airline departures a day. Air China has the most (46), followed by Hainan Airlines (8).

Air China flies 83% of the 51,318 seats leaving PEK each day. Air China has 475% more gates than Hainan Airlines. 51 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
