How many gates does Helsinki Airport (HEL) have?

Helsinki Airport (HEL) has 55 gates, with about 222 airline departures a day. Finnair has 34 gates.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 9, 2026 · 3 full days from Nov 1.

Finnair flies 35% of the 7,888 seats leaving HEL each day. 12 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
