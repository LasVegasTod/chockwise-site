How many gates does Lima Airport (LIM) have?

Lima Airport (LIM) has 48 gates, with about 263 airline departures a day. LATAM Perú has the most (15), followed by American (1).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 9, 2026 · 3 full days from Nov 1.

LATAM Perú flies 0% of the 3,922 seats leaving LIM each day. LATAM Perú has 15.0 times as many gates as American. 30 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
