How many gates does Tokyo Haneda Airport (HND) have?

Tokyo Haneda Airport (HND) has 108 gates, with about 668 airline departures a day. All Nippon Airways has the most (26), followed by Japan Airlines (25).

All Nippon Airways flies 48% of the 140,979 seats leaving HND each day. All Nippon Airways has 4% more gates and 36% more seats than Japan Airlines. 10 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
