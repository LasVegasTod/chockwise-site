How many gates does Bali Airport (DPS) have?

Bali Airport (DPS) has 15 gates, with about 196 airline departures a day. 15 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 23, 2026 · 10 full days from Oct 27.

Jetstar flies 42% of the 8,039 seats leaving DPS each day. 15 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
