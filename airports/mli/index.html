How many gates does Moline/Quad Cities Airport (MLI) have?

Moline/Quad Cities Airport (MLI) has 13 gates, with about 16 airline departures a day. United has the most (4), followed by American (3).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 1, 2026 · 26 full days from Oct 6.

United flies 31% of the 1,022 seats leaving MLI each day. United has 33% more gates than American.
