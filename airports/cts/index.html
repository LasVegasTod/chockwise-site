How many gates does Sapporo Airport (CTS) have?

Sapporo Airport (CTS) has 27 gates, with about 231 airline departures a day. All Nippon Airways has the most (4), followed by Japan Airlines (3).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 1, 2026 · 25 full days from Oct 8.

All Nippon Airways flies 41% of the 33,124 seats leaving CTS each day. All Nippon Airways has 33% more gates and 72% more seats than Japan Airlines. 18 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
