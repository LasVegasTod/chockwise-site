How many gates does Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI) have?

Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI) has 58 gates, with about 370 airline departures a day. Iberia has the most (4), followed by Ryanair (2).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 2, 2026 · 26 full days from Oct 8.

Iberia flies 0% of the 30,982 seats leaving PMI each day. Iberia has 2.0 times as many gates as Ryanair. 46 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
