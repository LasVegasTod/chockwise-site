How many gates does Mexico City Airport (MEX) have?

Mexico City Airport (MEX) has 71 gates, with about 380 airline departures a day. Aeroméxico has the most (24), followed by Viva Aerobus (3).

Aeroméxico flies 31% of the 37,742 seats leaving MEX each day. Aeroméxico has 700% more gates and 260% more seats than Viva Aerobus. 32 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
