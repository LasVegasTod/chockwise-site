How many gates does Málaga Airport (AGP) have?

Málaga Airport (AGP) has 30 gates, with about 261 airline departures a day. 28 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 9, 2026 · 3 full days from Nov 1.

Ryanair flies 49% of the 31,010 seats leaving AGP each day. 28 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
