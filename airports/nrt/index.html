How many gates does Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT) have?

Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT) has 86 gates, with about 303 airline departures a day. All Nippon Airways and Jetstar Japan have the most, 9 each.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Oct 5, 2026 · 3 full days from Oct 3.

All Nippon Airways flies 12% of the 35,250 seats leaving NRT each day. 50 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
