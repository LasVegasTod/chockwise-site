How many gates does Mumbai Airport (BOM) have?

Mumbai Airport (BOM) has 66 gates, with about 393 airline departures a day. IndiGo has 12 gates.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 16, 2026 · 16 full days from Oct 20.

IndiGo flies 0% of the 5,100 seats leaving BOM each day. 49 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
