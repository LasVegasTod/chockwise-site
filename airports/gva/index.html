How many gates does Geneva Airport (GVA) have?

Geneva Airport (GVA) has 32 gates, with about 208 airline departures a day. easyJet has the most (3), followed by SWISS (1).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 2, 2026 · 2 full days from Nov 1.

easyJet flies 58% of the 24,459 seats leaving GVA each day. easyJet has 3.0 times as many gates and 6.0 times as many seats as SWISS. 26 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
