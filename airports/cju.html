How many gates does Jeju Airport (CJU) have?

Jeju Airport (CJU) has 24 gates, with about 250 airline departures a day. Jeju Air, Eastar Jet and Jin Air have the most, 1 each.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 2, 2026 · 26 full days from Oct 8.

Jeju Air flies 22% of the 32,841 seats leaving CJU each day. Jeju Air has 53% more seats than Eastar Jet. 21 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
