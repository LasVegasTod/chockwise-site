How many gates does Brussels Airport (BRU) have?

Brussels Airport (BRU) has 57 gates, with about 176 airline departures a day. Brussels Airlines has the most (26), followed by United (4).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 5, 2026 · 4 full days from Nov 1.

Brussels Airlines flies 0% of the 8,132 seats leaving BRU each day. Brussels Airlines has 6.5 times as many gates as United. 19 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
