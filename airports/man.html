How many gates does Manchester Airport (MAN) have?

Manchester Airport (MAN) has 66 gates, with about 315 airline departures a day. Ryanair has the most (14), followed by easyJet (10).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 2, 2026 · 26 full days from Oct 8.

Ryanair flies 38% of the 31,051 seats leaving MAN each day. Ryanair has 40% more gates and 6% more seats than easyJet. 30 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
