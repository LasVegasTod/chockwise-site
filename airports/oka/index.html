How many gates does Okinawa Airport (OKA) have?

Okinawa Airport (OKA) has 20 gates, with about 196 airline departures a day. All Nippon Airways has the most (4), followed by Japan Airlines (1).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 9, 2026 · 2 full days from Nov 2.

All Nippon Airways flies 45% of the 25,736 seats leaving OKA each day. All Nippon Airways has 4.0 times as many gates and 2.3 times as many seats as Japan Airlines. 13 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
