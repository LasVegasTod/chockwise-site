How many gates does Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) have?

Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) has 86 gates, with about 527 airline departures a day. Thai Airways has the most (9), followed by Bangkok Airways (3).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 2, 2026 · 26 full days from Oct 8.

Thai Airways flies 0% of the 19,928 seats leaving BKK each day. Thai Airways has 3.0 times as many gates as Bangkok Airways. 69 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
