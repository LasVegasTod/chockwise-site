How many gates does Phuket Airport (HKT) have?

Phuket Airport (HKT) has 11 gates, with about 156 airline departures a day. 10 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 9, 2026 · 2 full days from Nov 2.

Emirates flies 20% of the 4,183 seats leaving HKT each day. 10 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.

    How many flights a day leave Phuket Airport (HKT)?

    Which gates at Phuket Airport (HKT) are bus gates?

    8 gates at HKT are bus gates: 81, 82, 83, 83A, 83B, 84, 84A, 84B. A bus gate has no jet bridge: passengers board a bus at the gate, and it drives them out to the plane parked on the ramp. About 62 departures a day (40% of all departures at HKT) board this way (Nov 2 to Nov 9, 2026).

    How many seats leave HKT each day?

    About 4,183 seats on 156 airline departures a day (average of 2 full days, Nov 2 to Nov 9, 2026).

    What is the typical turn time at HKT?

    57 minutes at the gate from arrival to departure (median of every turn measured).
