How many gates does Stockholm Airport (ARN) have?

Stockholm Airport (ARN) has 65 gates, with about 270 airline departures a day. SAS has the most (11), followed by Ryanair (1).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 23, 2026 · 10 full days from Oct 27.

SAS flies 36% of the 18,456 seats leaving ARN each day. SAS has 11.0 times as many gates as Ryanair, and 51% more seats. 26 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
