How many gates does Málaga Airport (AGP) have?

Málaga Airport (AGP) has 30 gates, with about 242 airline departures a day. Ryanair has 1 gate.

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 23, 2026 · 5 full days from Nov 1.

Ryanair flies 50% of the 29,428 seats leaving AGP each day. 28 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
