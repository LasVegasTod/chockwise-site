How many gates does Copenhagen Airport (CPH) have?

Copenhagen Airport (CPH) has 89 gates, with about 387 airline departures a day. SAS has the most (27), followed by Ryanair (2).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 23, 2026 · 10 full days from Oct 27.

SAS flies 33% of the 29,640 seats leaving CPH each day. SAS has 13.5 times as many gates as Ryanair, and 88% more seats. 31 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
