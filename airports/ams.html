How many gates does Amsterdam Airport (AMS) have?

Amsterdam Airport (AMS) has 120 gates, with about 699 airline departures a day. KLM has the most (29), followed by easyJet (14).

KLM flies 21% of the 33,095 seats leaving AMS each day. KLM has 107% more gates than easyJet. 68 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
