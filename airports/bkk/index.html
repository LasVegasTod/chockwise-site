How many gates does Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) have?

Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) has 86 gates, with about 533 airline departures a day. Thai Airways has the most (8), followed by Bangkok Airways (3).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 16, 2026 · 16 full days from Oct 20.

Thai Airways flies 0% of the 20,004 seats leaving BKK each day. Thai Airways has 2.7 times as many gates as Bangkok Airways. 70 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
