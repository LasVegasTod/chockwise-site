How many gates does Osaka Kansai Airport (KIX) have?

Osaka Kansai Airport (KIX) has 61 gates, with about 258 airline departures a day. Peach has the most (15), followed by Jeju Air (3).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 23, 2026 · 10 full days from Oct 27.

Peach flies 26% of the 28,818 seats leaving KIX each day. Peach has 5.0 times as many gates and 3.6 times as many seats as Jeju Air. 38 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
