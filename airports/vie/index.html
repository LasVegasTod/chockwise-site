How many gates does Vienna Airport (VIE) have?

Vienna Airport (VIE) has 57 gates, with about 351 airline departures a day. Austrian has the most (30), followed by Ryanair (5).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 9, 2026 · 22 full days from Oct 13.

Austrian flies 0% of the 8,726 seats leaving VIE each day. Austrian has 6.0 times as many gates as Ryanair. 17 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
