How many gates does Madrid Airport (MAD) have?

Madrid Airport (MAD) has 118 gates, with about 659 airline departures a day. Iberia has the most (30), followed by Ryanair (4).

A gate counts as an airline’s when it (with its regional partners) flies at least 70% of the arrivals and departures there; gates no airline dominates are common use. Data as of Nov 2, 2026 · 28 full days from Oct 6.

Iberia flies 33% of the 81,637 seats leaving MAD each day. Iberia has 7.5 times as many gates and 2.3 times as many seats as Ryanair. 81 gates are common use, shared by several airlines.
