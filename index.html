What’s in the dataset

Gate ownership

Every gate drawn on the airport map and colored by the airline that holds it: exclusive, preferential, or common use.

Gate utilization

How long each gate is occupied through the operating day, turn by turn, from real arrivals and departures.

Turns and tows

Turn times at the gate, and the tows on and off gates each airline makes, airport by airport and day by day.

Seats and capacity

Seats in and out of every gate and airline each day, from each airline’s own seat map for each aircraft type.

Aircraft fit

The largest aircraft each gate has handled, from regional jets to wide-bodies, so you know where a 787 can actually park.
