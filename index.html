What we build

Gate ownership maps

Every gate drawn on the airport map and colored by the airline that holds it: exclusive, preferential or common use.

Gate utilization

How long each gate is occupied through the operating day, turn by turn, from real arrivals and departures.

Turns and tows

Aircraft turn times at the gate, and the tows on and off gates each airline makes, airport by airport and day by day.
