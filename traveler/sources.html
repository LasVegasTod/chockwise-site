Where Chockwise Traveler listings come from

OpenStreetMap

Many listings and every airport map on Chockwise come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. If you see something missing or wrong, you can fix it in OpenStreetMap for everyone.

Foursquare Open Source Places

Other listings come from Foursquare Open Source Places, licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Contains data from Foursquare Open Source Places (release of October 15, 2026). Copyright Foursquare Labs, Inc.

How Chockwise uses them

Each place is matched to the nearest gate on Chockwise’s own gate map, and grouped by the terminal or concourse that gate is in (from flight data). Listings at closed terminals, staff rooms, places with no name and Foursquare entries not refreshed since before 2024 are left out, and listings flagged for review are held back. Walk times are estimates: the straight-line distance on the map plus a quarter, at an average pace of 1.2 metres a second. Our sources rarely say whether a place is before or after security, or its hours, so we don’t claim them. Every listing is marked unverified.
