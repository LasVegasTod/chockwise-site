How are the LAS walk times worked out?

From the distance between your gate and each place on Chockwise’s gate map, plus a quarter for the walk round, at 1.2 m/s. They are estimates, not measured routes; allow longer with children or heavy bags.

Where these listings come from. Places are from OpenStreetMap and Foursquare Open Source Places, matched to the nearest gate on Chockwise’s gate map. They are unverified: shops and restaurants open, close and move, and our sources don’t say which side of security each one is on.
