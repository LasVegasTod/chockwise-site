ONE LINE
Chockwise is an AI airport gate intelligence platform that maps how every gate is used, from real flight data, with no cameras to install.

50 WORDS
Chockwise is an AI airport gate intelligence platform founded by Todd Miller, a 787 captain at a major U.S. airline. It maps every gate at 266 airports from real flight data and briefs airlines and airports on how those gates are used. No cameras or hardware. chockwise.com

FOUNDER, SHORT BIO
Todd Miller is a 787 captain at a major U.S. airline, a former U.S. Marine Corps A-6 pilot, and a U.S. Naval Academy graduate. He is the Founder of Chockwise, an AI platform that maps how every airport gate is used, and of GateBite, a free traveler app for food and coffee near your gate. He lives in Las Vegas.
