Busiest gates in America

Original Chockwise research, ranked from public flight data.

Washington National (DCA) and Austin (AUS) have the busiest gates in America: about 7 airline departures a day from each gate. O’Hare, San Diego and Seattle are next, at about 6.

The ranking covers 51 U.S. airports with 25 or more gates in the country’s largest metro areas. The measure is airline departures per mapped gate per day, from public flight data, not yearly passengers per gate. The unweighted average across the 51 airports is 4.1 departures per gate per day. Las Vegas sits near the middle, at 4.5. Data as of November 20, 2026.

About Chockwise

Chockwise started in the cockpit.

Todd Miller is a 787 captain at a major U.S. airline. After a departure delay, his crew will often make up the time in the air to save passengers’ connections, sometimes the difference between getting home and a night in the airport. Then they land, and the gate isn’t available. The deeper problem is familiar on the ground too: some airlines underuse their gates, while others trying to add service are capped by gate access. That costs airports landing-fee revenue, and it persists because it is hard to see how every gate is actually used.

So he built Chockwise. It maps every gate at 266 airports from real arrival and departure data: who uses it, how hard it works, when it sits empty, and the turns, tows and seats that move through it. An AI analyst answers questions in plain English and writes weekly briefings for airline network planners, station managers, ground ops teams and airports. There are no cameras and no hardware to install.

Chockwise is based in Las Vegas, Nevada, and is a product of Chockwise LLC. Its sister consumer product is GateBite (gatebite.com), a free website and iPhone app for finding food and coffee near an airport gate. GateBite’s press page is at gatebite.com/press.

Founder

Todd Miller, Founder

Todd Miller is the Founder of Chockwise LLC, based in Las Vegas. He is a 787 captain at a major U.S. airline, a former U.S. Marine Corps A-6 pilot (Desert Storm), and a U.S. Naval Academy graduate. He has been flying for 40 years: 10 years in the U.S. Marine Corps and 30-plus years at his current airline.

He previously co-founded Homing In, an agent-sourced home value tool for homeowners, and ReferralCloud, a real estate clearinghouse where agents find other agents to refer clients to. He also built GateBite (gatebite.com), a free website and iPhone app for finding food and coffee near an airport gate.

He founded Chockwise after too many flights where his crew made up a delay to save passengers’ connections, only to land and wait for a gate.

What it does

  • Gate utilization, turn times, tows and idle gaps from real flight data, for every gate at the airports we cover
  • A plain-English AI analyst and weekly written briefings
  • Gate-allocation what-ifs and airline scorecards
  • No cameras or hardware; a software and data product that can be rolled out across many airports
  • Built for network planners, station managers, airport commercial and strategy teams, and ground ops, not another day-of-operations control system
